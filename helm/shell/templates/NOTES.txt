{{- if .Values.extensions.enabled }}

Extensions are being MOUNTED from a volume, not baked into the image. The volume
is mounted read-only over /app/wwwroot/extensions and must already contain an
assembled dist — registry.json plus one folder per extension, exactly what
`node scripts/build-all.mjs` writes to extensions/dist/.

  Use the plain shell image in this mode. Mounting over /app/wwwroot/extensions
  shadows any extensions baked into a layered (shell-ext) image: the volume is
  served and the baked assets become unreachable, with nothing logged about it.

Check what the pod is actually serving:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "shell.fullname" . }} -- ls /app/wwwroot/extensions

An empty or absent volume is not an error. The registry fetch soft-fails and the
sidebar shows only built-in tools, so verify the mount if extensions go missing.

Extensions, if any, come from the image itself (see Dockerfile.extensions). To
